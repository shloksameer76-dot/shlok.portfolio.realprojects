Client Hyperlab
Role Product flow and concept UI
Timeline 3 months

A physical intelligent laser-equipped interactive training companion that turns any space into a data-driven performance lab, powering the future of athletic training.

Hyperlab is building a full-stack Physical Intelligence platform that combines purpose-built hardware, computer vision, and a data-driven reporting layer to measure, understand, and improve human performance. Across schools and sports academies, students complete a series of scientifically benchmarked physical tests using Hyperlab's device ecosystem — Surge M47, ATOMEL Helio, Axis, and Pulse. Each device captures high-fidelity metrics such as rep depth, reaction time, HR recovery, acceleration curves, and balance stability.

Visit Website

The Ask

Hyperlab approached us to modernize the platform's visual language and deliver a cleaner, more polished interface.

Reframing the Problem

Discovery revealed a deeper gap: despite sophisticated hardware and protocols, Hyperlab lacked a reporting layer to interpret its data.

Users couldn't understand scores, abilities, or progress — and future features couldn't scale without a clear interpretation model.

Through focused stakeholder sessions, we demonstrated why simple, trustworthy, and well-structured data representation was essential. By showing how complex insights could be distilled into a clear, intuitive flow, we aligned the team around a new direction.

The mandate shifted from a visual refresh to creating Hyperlab's reporting framework — a foundational layer that would define how the platform communicates performance and drives the next generation of the product.

Muscle Strength Report

The Muscle Strength Report page consolidates all key strength data into a clear, insight-driven snapshot. It features the Muscle Strength Analysis graph to visualise ascent–descent patterns and test rhythm, a simplified Push-Up Score for quick performance interpretation, and Average Push-Up Time insights that reveal pacing, control, and fatigue trends — creating a structured, intuitive view that helps students and coaches quickly understand overall strength performance.

Performance Overview

The flow starts with a Performance Overview that introduces Hyperlab's first top-level Physical Intelligence summary, creating an immediate, high-value insight that helps users understand overall performance before exploring deeper details.

Domain Strength

Domain Strength Section, which standardises the eight core abilities into a consistent performance fingerprint, and Domain Deep Dives that explain what each ability measures and how it changes across tests.
