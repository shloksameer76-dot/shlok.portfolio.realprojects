About

Building the future where technology feels human.

Product Designer at the intersection of technology, AI, and product thinking — building systems that simplify complexity, align human needs with business outcomes, and hold up in real-world use.

Growing up in Thane and studying across Australia, Italy, and the UK shaped how I think about design and how people interact with technology across contexts.

My work goes beyond interfaces into systems and behaviour. I use emerging technologies and vibe-coded workflows to explore ideas quickly, treating prototypes as tools to think with, not just present.

I collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, contribute to product direction, and make decisions based on clarity and outcomes — especially in ambiguous situations.

I build independently too, using AI to explore directions faster and expand what's possible. What drives me is building products that challenge how things are done and evolve from static tools into adaptive, intelligent systems.
