I design products at the intersection of technology, design, and product thinking. My work is divided into two parts, Projects and Labs.

Projects reflects real, shipped products shaped by users, constraints, and business impact. It represents execution, working within real-world complexity to deliver outcomes that are usable, scalable, and valuable.

Labs is where I step beyond those constraints. It is an experimental space where I build vibe-coded software to explore how future products evolve. Unlike traditional projects, Labs is not driven by fixed briefs or predefined outcomes. Instead, it is driven by curiosity, iteration, and the need to understand what comes next.

Me

Product Designer fueled by ambition, working at the intersection of technology, AI, and product thinking, shaping how future products behave, evolve, and scale. I build systems that simplify complexity, align human needs with business outcomes, and hold up in real-world use. Growing up in Thane and studying across Australia, Italy, and the UK shaped how I think about design and how people interact with technology across contexts.

My work goes beyond interfaces into systems and behaviour. I use emerging technologies and vibe-coded workflows to explore ideas quickly, treating prototypes as tools to think with, not just present. I don't follow a linear process. I move between understanding the problem and shaping the solution, going back often to realign and get closer to what actually matters.

I work closely with cross-functional teams, contribute to product direction, and make decisions based on clarity and outcomes, especially in ambiguous situations. Alongside this, I spend time building independently, using AI to explore directions faster and expand what's possible.

What drives me is building products that challenge how things are done, push beyond the obvious, and move from static tools into systems that adapt, respond, and improve with use.
