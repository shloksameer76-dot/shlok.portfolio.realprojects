User & Market Context

Banking today is largely optimized for transactional efficiency, while users manage their finances alongside work, health, shopping, and long-term life decisions. This fragmentation forces users to switch between multiple tools to stay in control. The primary users are digitally mature professionals and small business owners who expect intelligent guidance, not manual tracking. For them, the gap is not access to features, but the lack of a system that understands context, anticipates needs, and supports daily decision-making.

The Ask

Design a next-generation banking experience that meaningfully increases daily active usage by going beyond transactions and positioning the bank as an intelligent, everyday companion.

Product Framing

The opportunity was to rearchitect banking apps beyond transactional utilities, introducing a new interaction paradigm for continuous, intent-driven engagement beyond traditional finance.

By shifting banking from episodic, task-based usage to a proactive interaction layer, the experience embeds finance into daily routines — leveraging AI to surface timely, relevant guidance across financial and lifestyle contexts.

Reframing the challenge

The challenge was to leverage AI as the primary interaction layer to drive higher DAU and user acquisition, shifting banking from low-frequency, transaction-led usage to a habit-forming, intent-driven engagement model that scales across user segments.

Strategic Interaction Shifts

The experience was designed around three fundamental shifts in how users engage with banking:

1. Transaction-Centric → Intent-Oriented
Users think in goals and routines, not balances. The AI layer interprets contextual signals to surface relevant actions without requiring explicit search or navigation.

2. Event-Triggered → Continuous Engagement
The product moves beyond isolated financial moments to engage users across daily touchpoints, establishing habit loops that drive consistent usage.

3. Feature Navigation → Adaptive Interaction Layer
The homepage evolves into a conversational, dynamic interface that prioritizes what matters in the moment — reducing cognitive load and enabling fluid, human-centered interactions.

The Intelligent Daily Control Center
The Super App for Everyday Life

A unified, AI-driven daily overview that brings together finance and health insights, business transactions, insurance status, wealth performance, relevant shopping and health-related offers, and upcoming decisions like car loans, auto insurance, and investment planning. By consolidating these signals into a single, contextual entry point, the experience helps users start their day with clarity — surfacing what needs attention, what requires action, and what opportunities lie ahead, without navigating multiple products or screens.

Finance & Health Insights

Designed to help users start the day with clarity, this experience connects financial signals with health context in a single view. By intelligently surfacing balances, spending patterns, upcoming obligations, and relevant health cues, it reduces cognitive load and enables more grounded, day-to-day decisions.

Business Transactions & Insurance Insights

Built to minimize operational risk and manual effort, this layer proactively manages business payments, revenue insights, investment opportunities, and insurance status. Critical actions and alerts are surfaced at the right moment, helping users stay ahead of deadlines, optimize capital, and maintain coverage with confidence.

Smart Wealth Management

Focused on long-term growth without constant monitoring, the system translates complex portfolio data into clear, timely guidance. By tracking performance, liquidity, and market signals, it recommends when to invest, rebalance, or hold — making wealth management more intuitive and less reactive.

Shopping & Health-Related Offers

This experience delivers relevance over volume. By combining spending behavior, lifestyle patterns, and health context, it surfaces offers only when they align with real user needs — turning promotions into value-adding recommendations rather than interruptions.
